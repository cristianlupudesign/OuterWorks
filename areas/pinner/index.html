Straight answers

Fencing in Pinner: what people ask us.

Do I need permission for a new fence in Pinner?

Usually not. Under national permitted-development rules a garden fence can go up to 2 metres high, or 1 metre where it borders a road used by vehicles. If your home is in one of Pinner's conservation areas or is listed, we'll tell you at the survey whether Harrow Council should be asked first.

Can you get materials down a narrow side passage?

Yes — most Pinner semis have tight side access and we plan for it. Posts, panels and boards are carried through by hand and the old fence goes out the same way. Nothing gets left on your drive.

How quickly can you start in HA5?

Pinner is a short run from our Ruislip base, so we can usually survey within days. Your start date goes in writing at survey — if we're late on our side, you take £100 a day off the bill.
