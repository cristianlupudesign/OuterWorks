Straight answers

Fencing in Harrow: what people ask us.

What fence height is allowed in Harrow?

Without planning permission a garden fence can be up to 2 metres high, or 1 metre next to a road used by vehicles. Harrow on the Hill and some other parts of the borough are conservation areas — we'll flag at the survey if your property needs a check with Harrow Council.

Do you cover Wealdstone and Kenton?

Yes. All of HA1, HA2 and HA3 is in our usual patch, including Wealdstone, Kenton, Harrow Weald, North Harrow and South Harrow. Send your postcode and we'll confirm the same working day.

Can you replace just one side of the garden?

Yes. We price per metre, so a single boundary is no problem. Minimum job value is £750, and the price is fixed in writing at survey.
