Straight answers

Fencing in Ruislip: what people ask us.

Do I need permission for a new fence in Ruislip?

Usually not. A back garden fence can be up to 2 metres high without planning permission, or 1 metre next to a road used by vehicles. Ruislip Village and some nearby streets are conservation areas — we'll flag at the survey if Hillingdon Council should be asked first.

More detail: do you need planning permission for a fence? · which fence is yours?

How quickly can you start in HA4?

Ruislip is our home patch, so we can usually survey within days. Your start date goes in writing — if we're late on our side, you take £100 a day off the bill.

Our fence blew down in a storm. Can you replace it?

Yes. We clear the damaged panels and posts, then rebuild on posts set to last. Storm-damaged runs are often a post problem, not a panel problem, and we fix the cause.
