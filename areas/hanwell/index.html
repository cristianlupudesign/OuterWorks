Straight answers

Fencing in Hanwell: what people ask us.

Do I need permission for a new fence in Hanwell?

Usually not. A back garden fence can be up to 2 metres high without planning permission, or 1 metre next to a road used by vehicles. Some parts of Hanwell are conservation areas, so we'll check your street at the survey.

More detail: do you need planning permission for a fence? · which fence is yours?

Can you do a front garden fence?

Yes. Front boundaries next to a road are normally limited to 1 metre without permission. We'll help you pick a style that looks right from the street and stays within the rules.

Can you replace just one side of the garden?

Yes. We price per metre, so one boundary is no problem, and the price is fixed in writing at the survey.
