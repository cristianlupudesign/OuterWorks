What we do

A gate is only as good as its posts.

Most gates that drag, drop or won't latch aren't bad gates — they're hung on posts that have moved. A gate puts a lot of leverage on one post, so we set the hanging post deep and solid before the gate goes anywhere near it.

We build or supply the gate to suit the opening and match it to the fence either side, so the boundary reads as one job. It's surveyed and priced in writing before we start.

  • Hanging posts set deep — so the gate doesn't drop or bind.
  • Matched to the fence — same boards, same line, same finish.
  • Proper ironmongery — heavy hinges, a latch that lines up, a bolt or lock if you want one.
  • Sized to the opening — room for bins, bikes or a mower to get through.

Straight answers

Good to know about gates.

My gate drags on the ground. Does it need replacing?

Not always. If the gate itself is sound, the problem is usually the hanging post leaning or the hinges working loose. We'll look at it at the survey and tell you straight whether it's a re-hang or a new gate.

Can you match a gate to my existing fence?

Yes. We match the board type, height and finish so the gate sits in line with the fence either side. If the fence is being replaced too, we build both together.

Can you fit a lock?

Yes — a padlockable latch, a bolt on the inside, or a lock that opens from both sides. Tell us how you use the gate and we'll fit the right one.
